# SAFAQAT approved decisions

## Contact verification - 2026-11-26

- Phone verification is mandatory for all four account roles.
- Gmail and email are optional for all four roles.
- Explain email as additional security and easier account recovery.
- Allow email to be added later through account settings.
- Preserve approved identity verification requirements.
- Never simulate successful verification.
- Public registration remains disabled until database constraints,
  provider integration and security validation are complete.
- Preserve approved landing artwork and Arabic RTL design.
- Smart assistant implementation is deferred.

## Marketplace and store management

- Preserve all approved marketplace rules.
- Sort nearby stores and available couriers by distance.
- Show minimum order, delivery availability and payment methods.
- Support cash, mobile wallets, InstaPay and Fawry where available.
- Require explicit location sharing and revoke delivery access
  when the delivery is completed.
- Preserve merchant transaction history and consumer monthly
  consumption records.
- Build integrated POS, barcode scanning, inventory management,
  shortages, returns, receipt printing and sales reports.
- Synchronize authorized store computers and owner phones.
- Implement Owner Mode with server-enforced permissions.
- Support secure owner authentication and independent employees.
- Record shifts, attendance, sales and cashier balances.
- Use red attendance indicators for late arrivals and green
  indicators for early or on-time arrivals.
- No employee screen monitoring or recording.

## Financial services

- Plan wallets for all four account roles.
- Support available, pending and reserved balances.
- Plan mobile wallet, InstaPay and Fawry integrations.
- Require authorized payment providers before real transactions.
- Never collect provider PINs or OTPs.
- Confirm withdrawal support before enabling each provider.

## Smart transport and deferred assistant

- Motorcycle pricing: EGP 6 per kilometer, EGP 15 minimum.
- Confirm road distance before issuing final quotations.
- Approach distance pricing remains unresolved.
- Other vehicle prices remain unresolved.
- Passenger transport requires legal and safety approval.
- Preserve the smart assistant concept for all four roles.
- Voice ordering, shortage collection, supplier comparison,
  offers, payment methods, delivery and invoice preparation.
- Smart assistant implementation is deferred.

## Infrastructure

- Preserve approved visual identity.
- Keep main unchanged.
- Use sequential low-memory validation.
- Do not activate paid services without approval.
- Publish validated and authorized changes only.
